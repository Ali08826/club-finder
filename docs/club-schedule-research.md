## Evidence and status rules

- **Verified:** Current-semester evidence confirms the **same named organization**, recurrence, day, **start and end** time; location separately marked confirmed or TBD.
- **Unverified:** Insufficient evidence to certify the original record or a full schedule. This includes start-only announcements and ambiguous generic labels.
- **Variable:** Schedules are naturally team-, course section-, week-, event- or fixture-dependent. A historical schedule may still be supplied as context.
- **Outdated:** A located schedule explicitly applies to a past term or year, not Fall 2026.
- `TBD` is unknown, **not** the prototype sample. Do not put incomplete or historical observations into the live conflict checker.
- Check date is the date of web review, **not** the date a club officer confirmed the schedule. Source pages may change after research.

## D. Engineering / implementation recommendations

1. **Keep `id` stable** for bookmarks and `localStorage`; preserve existing sessions under a clearly labeled `sampleSessions` key or a separate source snapshot, never as `verifiedSessions`.
2. Add `entityType`, `scheduleMode` (`fixed`/`team_specific`/`event_based`/`unknown`), `status`, `sourceUrl`, `checkedAt`, `semester`, `evidenceScope`, and `notes` fields.
3. A verified interval requires day, start, end, same entity, source, and semester. **Do not fabricate duration** from a start-only announcement.
4. Show `Schedule TBD` or `Varies by team/event` instead of sample intervals. Only validated current-semester intervals enter weekly grid and collision detection.
5. Turn category placeholders (`invest`, `speak`, `code`) into **explicitly named clubs** after teammate review, preserving original IDs if continuity is intended. Suggested mappings: Investment & Trading at Purdue, Toastmasters at Purdue, Purdue Hackers. Mapping is a proposal, not verification.
6. For VIP/EPICS, use a parent program record with child team/section meeting records; for Grand Prix and intramurals, use team/event/fixture records.
7. Re-check published information at semester start and set `expiresAt` at term end; do not label 2024/2026 spring data current indefinitely.
8. **Important API distinction:** `status=Variable` describes schedule structure, not evidence quality. In a future version, separate `scheduleMode` from `evidenceStatus` so a particular VIP section could be both team-specific and verified.
